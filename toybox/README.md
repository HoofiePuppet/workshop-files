# Toy Box

Toys from Hoofie Puppet's Workshop, inspired by the wind-up and crank-driven mechanical toys of the late 1800s.
**Designer:** Hoofie Puppet · **License:** CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (see the repo's LICENSE.txt)
**On the BBS:** [Toy Box](https://hoofiepuppet.neocities.org/toybox.html)

## In progress: De-Susi No. 1, Inés and Her Abaniko

A wind-up automaton in the late-Victorian style: a young Filipina in a María Clara gown who dances the Cariñosa, hiding behind her fan, peeking out at you, then lowering it with a smile. Fully mechanical, with a spring motor, cams, levers and a 30-note music box. 1/4 scale (40 cm tall).

- **Done:** her head (removable magnetic pate), her song (the traditional Cariñosa arranged for the music box), and her eye mechanism. Her eyes glance left and right together on a small steering-style linkage held in by one screw.
- **Watch:** [her eyes glance (YouTube)](https://youtube.com/shorts/IYBUSyvcPr8)
- **Next:** her neck, so she can turn and tilt her head.

Her files will be posted here in `toybox/ines/` once each part is tested and complete. The eye parts go up after they're fitted to the real glass eyes.

> **Small parts:** toys here contain small parts and are not for children under 3. Build and use at your own risk.

Each toy gets its own folder here: `toybox/<toy-name>/` with its files, photos and a README.
