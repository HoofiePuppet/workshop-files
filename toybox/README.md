# Toy Box

Toys from Hoofie Puppet's Workshop, inspired by the wind-up and crank-driven mechanical toys of the late 1800s.
**Designer:** Hoofie Puppet · **License:** CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (see the repo's LICENSE.txt)
**On the BBS:** [Toy Box](https://hoofiepuppet.neocities.org/toybox.html)

**Coming soon:** an old-fashioned automaton, a doll that moves and plays.

> **Small parts:** toys here contain small parts and are not for children under 3. Build and use at your own risk.

Each toy gets its own folder here: `toybox/<toy-name>/` with its files, photos and a README.
