**Status:** Released · **Build cost:** about ₱500 in total

| # | Item | Qty | Spec / notes |
|---|---|---|---|

| A3 | Aluminum heatsink | 1 | 40 × 40 × 11 mm |
| A4 | MT3608 DC-DC step-up boost converter | 1 | 2A. Output set to about 7–8 V for the Peltier. |
| A5 | 22mm wrist watch straps | 1 pair | |
| A6 | Portable charger (power bank) | 1 | 10,000mAh |
| A7 | USB cable with micro connector | 1 | Power bank → MT3608 |
| A8 | Thermal grease | small amount | Between the Peltier hot side and the heatsink |
| A9 | Epoxy | small amount | Lid A to the cage (one dab at each corner) |
| A10 | Hot glue | small amount | Lid B on the MT3608 box (removable later) |
