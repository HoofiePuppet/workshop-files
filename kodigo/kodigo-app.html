Your own Kodigo cloud (optional)

Kodigo cloud (for Tagalog and Taglish on iPhone) is shared by everyone, about 3½ hours a day in total. For your own free 3½ hours a day, set up your own in about 10 minutes (steps in the setup guide), then paste its address here.

How to use / install
  • iPhone: open this page in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen. It opens like an app. Safari will ask for the microphone and speech recognition the first time.
  • Android: open in Chrome, menu → Add to Home screen.
  • PC / Mac: works in Chrome or Edge with a microphone.
  • Listening: Automatic uses the phone's own fast speech service, and switches to Kodigo cloud (Whisper) for languages the phone can't do, like Tagalog on iPhone. Kodigo cloud captions arrive a phrase at a time, about 2–4 seconds behind, and are free up to about 3½ hours a day.
  • Saved log: every caption is saved on this phone only, with its time, and comes back when you reopen Kodigo. "Clear screen" only clears the screen; the log stays. Save as text file downloads the whole log with dates and times. On iPhone, open Kodigo from the home-screen icon so Safari doesn't wipe the log after 7 days without a visit.
  • Translate: pick the language people are speaking under Speech, and the language you read best under Translate to. The original words show small under each translated line.
  • Glasses: "Connect glasses" works in Chrome / Edge (PC, Mac, Android). iPhone needs the free WebBLE app, then this page in Safari. Not needed until the glasses are built.
  • Speech-to-text and translation need an internet connection. Expect about a second of delay.
